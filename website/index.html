Accounts that never mix.

Each profile is its own data folder, opened through the app's own switch for exactly this. Logins, extensions, chats and settings stay with the profile they belong to.

  • Your existing install is safeIt appears as the Main profile, opens exactly as before and can't be deleted.
  • Fresh or overflowStart a profile empty, or as an overflow of another: its settings, MCP servers, agents and skills come along, its login doesn't. Hit a limit, sign in with a second account, keep going.
  • Deletes go to the TrashNever a permanent wipe.

How each app is kept separate

Cursor--user-data-dir ~/.cursor_profiles/work

Cursor profiles also get a title bar in their own colour, so you can tell windows apart in Mission Control.

One API for every account.

Prism serves an OpenAI- and Anthropic-compatible endpoint on your machine. Point Claude Code, Codex, Claude Desktop or a chat app at it, and each request goes out with the API keys of the identity you choose. Type @work or @personal to switch in the middle of a chat.

  • Automatic switchingBy keyword, by app, or by time of day.
  • Overflow when you hit a limitRate-limited or out of credit, requests move to a fallback identity until it recovers.
  • Spend and budgetsCost per identity and model, with a monthly budget that warns, falls back or stops.
